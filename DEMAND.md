# The price of revealing a coordinate demand late

Alice has x in Aᴵ and Bob wants x(i). If a common message is produced before Alice knows i, equality of messages implies equality at every coordinate. All |A|^|I| inputs require distinct messages; sending x meets the bound. If Bob first sends i, Alice returns x(i), yielding at most |I| |A| transcripts. For eight bits this is 4 transmitted bits in two turns versus 8 in the one-way worst case. Both directions and the additional round are charged. Public randomness, tolerated errors and repeated demands change the contract.
